ABOUT OUR ORGANIZATION

Empowering Communities for a Better Tomorrow

Southern Conxs Bazar Development Forum (SCDF) is committed to supporting communities through education, healthcare, skills development and sustainable development initiatives.

Our goal is to create opportunities, improve quality of life and help build a stronger, healthier and more sustainable future for everyone.
